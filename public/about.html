About NoCharity

A comedic, mathematical lens on billionaire philanthropy and relative financial sacrifice, inspired by Gianmarco Soresi's standup comedy.

🎭 Comedic Origin & Inspiration

NoCharity is an independent project built on a comedic premise inspired by Gianmarco Soresi's standup joke: when a multi-billionaire donates millions of dollars to charity, it sounds monumental—until you compare it to what that same percentage represents out of an everyday worker's paycheck.

"When you have hundreds of billions of dollars, giving away half a percent doesn't change your grocery bill, your housing security, or your generational wealth. When you earn a median monthly wage, giving away that same fraction requires real everyday sacrifice."

The project is intended to be light-hearted and satirical. It uses humor to reframe astronomical dollar figures in relatable, everyday financial terms like cups of coffee, restaurant dinners, grocery bills, or daily work hours.

📊 Public Data & Reasonable Accuracy

We use publicly available information and attempt to present it as accurately as reasonably possible. Wealth figures and donation amounts are gathered from public regulatory disclosures (SEC Form 4 beneficial ownership filings, IRS Form 990-PF private foundation returns), verified philanthropic indexes (Forbes, Bloomberg Billionaires Index), and the Giving Pledge.

However, publicly reported wealth and donation figures can be incomplete, estimated, disputed, or later revised. Billionaire fortunes fluctuate daily with capital markets, and private giving records may not always be transparent or real-time.

Strict Attribution

We distinguish between direct personal giving and disbursements made by corporate balance sheets or tax-exempt private endowments.

Temporal Anchoring

Donations are evaluated against the billionaire's documented net worth at the approximate date of gift rather than current market peaks.

⚖️ Independence & Disclaimers

NoCharity is an entirely independent project. NoCharity is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the people, organizations, publications, or sources referenced.

These calculations are strictly for entertainment and informational context—not financial, investment, legal, tax, or charitable advice. No user financial data or salary information is ever stored, tracked, or sent to external servers; calculations take place strictly inside your browser.
