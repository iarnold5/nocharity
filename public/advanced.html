📖 Methodology & marginal utility
Scale Reality ▾

Scale vs. Marginal Utility

This tool calculates pure mathematical proportion. However, percentage does not equal sacrifice.

A billionaire who gives away 0.5% of a $100 billion fortune still retains $99.5 billion. Even in conservative fixed-income investments, that fortune generates tens of millions of dollars in surplus capital every single week, with zero impact on lifestyle, security, or generational luxuries.

In contrast, giving 0.5% of an average earner's net worth or monthly paycheck touches money reserved for rent, groceries, vehicle repairs, or children's emergency medical care.

Methodology & Data Quality

Phase 12 Rigor

The goal of NoCharity is not academic perfection. The goal is preventing NoCharity from confidently displaying nonsense. Every donation is audited against the 8 Essential Questions, strict validation constraints, and temporal wealth consistency checks.

📖 Full Standalone Methodology Page ↗

❓ The 8 Essential Questions

Every donation in NoCharity must have sufficient metadata to answer each of these questions before publication

1. WHO? Entity Identification

The canonical individual or entity associated with the philanthropic event (e.g., Warren Buffett, MacKenzie Scott).

2. WHAT? Gift Description

Specific instrument, cause, purpose, or grant program described in regulatory filings or recipient disclosures.

3. WHEN? Temporal Anchor

ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) or year. Temporal anchor for pairing with historical wealth snapshots.

4. HOW MUCH? Quantified Valuation

Positive numeric amount (> $0) with specified currency code (default USD) and preservation of any multi-source spreads.

5. FROM WHOM? Attribution Source

Exact funding source: Personal Direct, Foundation Endowment, Corporate Treasury, or Donor-Advised Fund (DAF).

6. TO WHOM? Recipient Verification

Identified grantee entity, 501(c)(3) public charity, university, research institute, or intermediary trust.

7. SOURCE? Primary Citation

Audited regulatory filings (SEC Form 4, IRS Form 990-PF, official grant reports) or corroborated journalistic investigations.

8. HOW CERTAIN? Confidence Level

Verification tier (VERIFIED, PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, DISPUTED) reflecting evidentiary backing and source independence.

📜 Methodological Standards & Rules

Key guardrails preventing distorted philanthropic parity calculations

1. Historical Wealth Matching

Gifts are evaluated strictly against documented wealth at the time of donation. A 2018 gift cannot silently use a 2026 valuation snapshot without explicitly marking it as approximate or unavailable.

2. Personal vs Organization Isolation

Foundation disbursements, corporate treasury giving, and DAF transfers are tracked for context but quarantined completely from individual sacrifice and parity calculations.

3. Multi-Source Discrepancies

If credible sources disagree on gift amounts, NoCharity does not silently pick one. Both figures and the spread are permanently retained and flagged with Grade D.

4. Pledge & Dispute Quarantine

Non-binding pledges (Giving Pledge) do not count as completed giving until legally fulfilled. Disputed gifts are strictly excluded from default rankings.

5. Public Records Limitations

Public disclosures only cover regulated gifts (SEC Form 4 stock transfers, IRS 990-PF grants). Private cash, anonymous DAFs, and non-US jurisdictions have inherent data blind spots.

6. Documented vs Real-World Giving

"Documented giving" is not total giving. A billionaire with $2B documented gave at least $2B, whereas an undocumented billionaire cannot be claimed to have given nothing. Coverage indicators clarify time horizons.
