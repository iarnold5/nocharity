1. No Account System or Personal Data Backend

NoCharity has no user account system. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or payment information. There is no user database, registration endpoint, or profiling mechanism on our servers.

You can use every feature of NoCharity anonymously without creating an identity.

2. Browser Local Storage (Client-Side Only)

To make the calculator convenient so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every time you refresh the page, NoCharity saves a few non-identifying preference keys to your browser's private localStorage sandbox.

These keys remain entirely on your personal device. They are never sent to our servers, sold, or shared with third parties:

Key Name Type Purpose Example Value
nocharity_income Number Stores your voluntary income or net worth figure for parity scaling 35000 or 4000
nocharity_currency String Stores your selected currency symbol ₹, $, €, or £
nocharity_income_frequency String Stores whether your entered income is monthly or annual "monthly" or "annual"
nocharity_mode String Stores your preferred parity comparison mode "networth" or "monthly"
nocharity_advanced_mode Boolean String Stores whether you toggled between Basic and Advanced Mode "true" or "false"

How to clear this data: You can wipe this data at any time by opening your browser's Developer Tools (Application → Storage → Clear site data) or by clearing your browser cache.

3. Ephemeral Financial Calculations

When you enter your personal financial numbers into the calculator:

  • In Basic Mode, calculations execute client-side inside your browser engine.
  • In Advanced Mode, historical parity calculations sent to /api/advanced/parity are computed in temporary server memory and immediately returned to your browser.

We never write your financial numbers to disk, to a database, or to any access log. Your personal financial figures vanish the moment your calculation response is sent.

4. Zero Cookies & Zero Third-Party Analytics

NoCharity sets zero HTTP cookies. We do not use session cookies, persistent tracking cookies, or flash cookies.

Furthermore, we do not bundle any analytics trackers, behavioral profiling scripts, advertisement networks, or telemetry trackers (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, or Facebook Pixel). We do not track you across websites or monitor your browsing behavior.

5. External Services & Requests

To render the website and display public philanthropic records, NoCharity interacts with a minimal set of external services:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com): Used to deliver typography (Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Cinzel, JetBrains Mono). Your browser requests these font files directly from Google's content delivery network, subject to Google's standard privacy policies.
  • Supabase (supabase.co): Houses the public, read-only database of verified billionaire wealth snapshots, donation receipts, and source references. All queries to Supabase fetch public reference data. No user identity or user financial numbers are ever sent to or stored in Supabase.
  • Google News RSS: In Advanced Mode, if you choose to search recent news coverage for a billionaire or use the discovery engine, our server fetches public RSS news headlines from Google News on-demand. These requests carry only the public figure's name and query parameters, never any user information.

6. Open-Source Verification

Because NoCharity is open source, every statement in this Privacy Policy is verifiable by anyone directly in our source code. You do not have to take our word for it—you can inspect our server routes, database queries, and client scripts directly on GitHub.
